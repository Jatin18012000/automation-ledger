Which mental skills does AI threaten most

About this ledger. Built from “Will AI Take My Job? Exposure, Skills & Wages” by Mind Lab on Kaggle. Exposure scores combine a human-rated LLM measure, a GPT-4-rated measure, and the AIOE index; cognitive ability data follows the O*NET taxonomy.
